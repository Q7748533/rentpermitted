Pennsylvania (PA) Short-Term Rental Regulations

State Overview

Pennsylvania has no state-level STR preemption law. Each city sets its own licensing, zoning, and tax requirements. Philadelphia is the only covered city, with a restrictive three-license framework. Other Pennsylvania STR markets (Pittsburgh, Harrisburg, Lancaster) may be added in future updates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pennsylvania have state-wide STR laws?

Pennsylvania regulates short-term rentals primarily at the city level. There is no comprehensive state-wide STR law. Philadelphia requires a three-license stack (CAL + Zoning Permit + Operator License) with strict primary residence rules for Limited Lodging.

Which Pennsylvania city is best for STR investment?

With Philadelphia as the covered city, owner-occupants can pursue Limited Lodging with manageable fees (~$300-500/yr). Non-owner-occupied Visitor Accommodation is heavily zone-restricted (CMX-3/4/5, CA-1/2, RMX-1/2 only). Philadelphia is primarily a Limited Lodging market.

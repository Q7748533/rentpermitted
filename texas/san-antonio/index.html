San Antonio, TX Short-Term Rental Regulations

Overview

San Antonio operates a two-tier STR permit system under Chapter 16, Article XXII of the City Code. Type 1 permits cover owner-occupied rentals at $300 per 3-year cycle. Type 2 permits allow non-owner-occupied rentals at $450 per 3-year cycle, but are subject to a 12.5% blockface density cap. Texas has no state-level STR preemption: cities retain full authority to require permits, set operating rules, and enforce penalties. The permit number must appear on all advertising platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, Booking.com). Applications are submitted through the BuildSA online portal (sanantonio.gov/DSD), with permits issued within 5 business days of a complete application.

One permit required per individual unit. Permits are non-transferable. +$10 processing fee for mail or in-person applications.

Taxes

Texas 6% state hotel occupancy tax + San Antonio 9% city hotel occupancy tax = ~15% total. Platforms (Airbnb, VRBO) typically collect and remit. Hosts must register with the Texas Comptroller for state hotel tax.

Key Operating Rules

  • Permit number must appear on all advertising platforms
  • Type 2: maximum 12.5% of units per blockface (one side of street between intersections)
  • Multi-family properties (5+ units): 12.5% of total units on property. One Type 2 by right regardless
  • Local contact must be available 24/7 for complaints
  • Noise and nuisance ordinances enforced
  • HOA rules may be more restrictive than city code
  • Renew every 3 years
  • Source: sanantonio.gov STR Permit Application (City Code Ch.16 Art.XXII)

Penalties for Non-Compliance

  • Operating without a permit: fines up to $2,000 per day per violation
  • Code enforcement monitors advertising platforms for unpermitted listings
  • Repeated violations may result in permit revocation
  • Source: San Antonio Code Enforcement, DSD

Enforcement Reality

San Antonio DSD actively monitors STR compliance through online platforms. The mandatory permit-display rule makes non-compliance easy to detect. Enforcement concentrated in high-density STR corridors like Riverwalk, Southtown, and Alamo Heights area.

Verdict

San Antonio offers a balanced STR investment environment. The 3-year permit cycle and absence of a primary residence requirement for Type 2 make it more accessible than Austin. The 12.5% blockface cap is the main constraint: desirable neighborhoods near the Riverwalk and Southtown may already be at capacity. With 35M+ annual visitors and diverse demand drivers (military, medical, tourism, conventions), San Antonio rewards investors who secure permits in unsaturated blocks. The ~15% tax rate is moderate for Texas markets.
